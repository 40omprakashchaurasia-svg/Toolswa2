Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Core Privacy Promise

At Toolswa (accessible from toolswa.com), the privacy and confidentiality of our visitors is our paramount priority. Unlike conventional cloud converters, Toolswa is built on a pure client-side execution model. When you use our PDF, Image, Word, or GST tools, your files are never uploaded to any remote server.

2. How Files Are Processed

All processing (including PDF merging, page splitting, image raster compression, QR generation, word counting, and tax calculations) is executed entirely within your browser's local sandbox memory using WebAssembly and standard JavaScript APIs. When you close the browser tab or hit "Reset", all temporary object URLs are automatically dereferenced and purged from your machine's volatile memory.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do NOT collect, inspect, or store your uploaded PDF documents or images.
  • We do NOT require user account creation, emails, or phone numbers to use the tools.
  • We do NOT track personal financial figures entered into the GST calculator.

4. Local Storage

Toolswa uses your browser's standard localStorage strictly to remember your UI display preferences: specifically your light/dark theme preference and your selected interface language (English or Hindi). No tracking cookies or personally identifiable identifiers are stored.

5. Contacting Us

If you have any questions or require more information regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at support@toolswa.com.
